## About this website

This website is an online catalogue. You can browse our product range, prices and details here, but purchases are completed in store, by phone, or by arrangement after you contact us — we do not take card payments through this website.

## Information we collect

### Information you give us

When you contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp, or our enquiry/contact form, we may collect:

- Your name and contact details (phone number, email address);
- The product(s) you are enquiring about and the contents of your message;
- Where you ask us to arrange an order or delivery, the delivery address and any details needed to fulfil it.

### Information we collect automatically

When you use the website we may automatically collect limited technical information such as your device type, browser, and how you use our pages. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies (see "Cookies" below).

## How we use your information

- To respond to your enquiries and provide the information or products you ask about;
- To arrange and fulfil orders, including dropship orders shipped directly from our suppliers, and any delivery you request;
- To operate, maintain and improve our website;
- To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

## Legal basis for processing

We rely on the following legal bases under GDPR: your **consent** (e.g. for non-essential cookies); the performance of a **contract** or steps taken at your request (e.g. arranging an order); our **legitimate interests** in running and improving our business and responding to enquiries; and compliance with a **legal obligation**.

## Cookies

Our website may use cookies to make the site work, remember your preferences, and help us understand how the site is used. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how the site works.

## Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only where necessary with:

- Suppliers and manufacturers, where needed to fulfil a dropship or special order you have placed;
- Delivery and courier partners, to deliver an order you have requested;
- Service providers who help us run our website and business (for example, hosting or analytics providers), under appropriate data-protection terms;
- Authorities or third parties where required by law.

## How long we keep your information

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out above, or as required to meet our legal, accounting or reporting obligations, after which it is securely deleted.

## Your rights

Under data protection law you have the right to: access the personal information we hold about you; have inaccurate data corrected; have your data erased; restrict or object to our processing; data portability; and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [info@eddiemaguire.ie](mailto:info@eddiemaguire.ie).

## Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can help. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish supervisory authority:

- **Data Protection Commission**, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28 — [www.dataprotection.ie](https://www.dataprotection.ie)
