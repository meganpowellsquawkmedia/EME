Overview

The Beko FDC6731S is a 60cm freestanding electric cooker in silver with a double oven and a 4-zone ceramic hob. Beko's AeroPerfect™ airflow keeps oven temperatures even for consistent baking, a full-width grill sits in the top cavity, and SimplySteam™ makes cleaning easier.
