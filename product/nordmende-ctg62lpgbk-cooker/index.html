Overview

The Nordmende CTG62LPGBK is a 60cm twin-cavity LPG (bottled-gas) cooker in black, pre-set LPG-ready so no conversion is needed. Four gas burners sit over a 62L main oven with a 33L top grill cavity. Enamel pan supports, easy-clean enamel and button ignition keep it simple. 3-year guarantee.

  • Pre-set LPG-ready — no conversion needed
  • Four gas burners
  • 62L main oven + 33L top grill cavity
  • Button ignition
  • Easy-clean enamel & enamel pan supports
  • Mechanical timer
  • Grill pan & 3 shelves included
  • 3-year guarantee
