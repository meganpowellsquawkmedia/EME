Overview

The Belling Cookcentre 110EBLK is a 110cm electric range cooker in black — a real kitchen centrepiece. A 65L main fan oven, 61L conventional second oven and separate 31L grill (157L total) pair with a five-zone ceramic hob featuring a dual zone and a warming zone.
